Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Pixel by Pixel Art is a one-person shop selling AI-made pixel art wallpapers. This page explains what information is handled when you visit the shop, buy something, or interact with our social accounts.

What we collect

  • Purchases: payments are processed by Stripe, PayPal or Gumroad. We never see or store your full card details. We receive your email address and order details so we can deliver your download and help if something goes wrong.
  • New-drop emails: if you subscribe, your email is held by Gumroad and used only to tell you about new collections. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Browsing: the shop doesn't use analytics trackers or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

Social media and API access

We use the official APIs of platforms such as Pinterest and YouTube only for our own accounts: to schedule our own posts and read our own analytics. We don't collect or store other users' data through these APIs.

How we use it

Only to deliver purchases, provide support, and send new-drop emails you asked for. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.
